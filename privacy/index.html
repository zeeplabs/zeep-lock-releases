Zeep Lock — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

In short: Zeep Lock keeps your passwords encrypted on your Mac. There is no account and no server of ours. We do not collect, receive or sell your data, and the app has no analytics, advertising or tracking. Two things can leave your Mac, and only if they apply to you: iCloud sync (optional, off by default, through your own iCloud account) and, in the version downloaded from our website, a daily check for updates.

1. Who we are

Zeep Lock is made by Zeep Labs, a brand of JULIO AUGUSTO MORAIS DE SOUSA DESENVOLVIMENTO DE SOFTWARE LTDA (CNPJ 37.818.214/0001-80), based in São Paulo, SP, Brazil (“we”). You can reach us about privacy at contato@zeeptecnologia.com.br.

2. Data stored by the app

Zeep Lock stores what you put in it: vault names, and for each credential the title, username, URL, password, notes and tags. This data stays on your Mac, encrypted with a key protected by your master password. Your master password is never stored and cannot be recovered by us or by anyone else.

We do not have access to this data. The app does not send it to us.

3. iCloud sync (optional)

Sync is off by default. If you turn it on, Zeep Lock stores an encrypted copy of your synced vaults in the private database of your own iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit. This copy is encrypted by the app before it leaves your Mac, so neither Apple nor we can read its contents. Apple's own terms and privacy policy apply to the iCloud service. You can stop sync, or delete the data from iCloud, in Settings ▸ iCloud. Vaults set to “This Mac only” are never uploaded.

Sync is a beta feature.

4. Updates

  • Mac App Store version: updates are delivered by the App Store. The app itself makes no update checks.
  • Version downloaded from our website: about once a day the app requests a small update feed from lock.zeeplabs.com.br, and downloads new versions from GitHub Releases when you choose to update. These requests are served by GitHub, which therefore sees your IP address and the app version, as with any website. GitHub's privacy statement applies. The app does not send a profile of your Mac, your passwords or any other data. Each update is verified with a cryptographic signature before it is installed.

5. Touch ID, clipboard and files

  • Touch ID is handled entirely by macOS. Zeep Lock never receives your fingerprint or any biometric data.
  • Clipboard: when you copy a password it stays on the clipboard for a limited time, is then cleared, and is kept out of the Universal Clipboard.
  • Exports and backups are files you create and control. An encrypted export is protected by the password you choose; an unencrypted export is not protected, and the app warns you before creating it.

6. What we do not do

We do not use analytics, crash-reporting or advertising services, do not track you across apps or websites, and do not share or sell data, because we do not have it.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your Mac until you delete it. You can delete credentials and vaults in the app, or erase everything from the lock screen (“Forgot your master password?”). Deleting the app does not by itself remove data stored in your iCloud account: use Settings ▸ iCloud ▸ Delete iCloud Data first if you used sync.

8. Your rights

Because we do not hold your data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side; you control it in the app. Under the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13,709/2018) you have rights regarding your personal data, including confirmation of processing, access, correction, deletion and information about sharing, and you may lodge a complaint with the Brazilian data protection authority (ANPD). For data stored by Apple or GitHub, contact them directly. For anything else, write to contato@zeeptecnologia.com.br.

9. Children

Zeep Lock is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect any data from them.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version at this address with a new date. If a change affects how the app treats your data, we will say so in the release notes.
